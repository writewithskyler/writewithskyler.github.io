It’s a cozy evening<br>
Warm shower, so fresh<br>
I was neither sad nor mad<br>
Songbirds were quiet<br>
At the shore near the tides

The night comes, a ghost rants<br>
I couldn’t sleep ’cause I’m scared<br>
But I told you that I am tired<br>
Took some pills and closed my eyes<br>
It’s even darker, I opened my eyes

This isn’t new, I already knew<br>
Bit my tongue, punched my face<br>
Screaming at the mirror, “Go to sleep!”<br>
It’s a daily task I need to do<br>
I’m so sleepy, but what can I do?
