title: "Nameless"
subtitle: "A poem about the masks we wear to be welcomed, and the self we lose beneath them."
description: "Nameless is a poem about wearing a mask to be admired and welcomed, and the quiet loss of self that comes with hiding who you really are."
---

I might have to change<br>
the thing that I speak<br>
the thing that I wear<br>
the thing that I feel<br>
to be admired

I might have to wear a mask<br>
to the place I go to study<br>
to the place I go to work<br>
to the place I thought I can be myself<br>
to be welcomed

I can't see anything clear<br>
when I look at myself in the mirror<br>
I couldn't breathe under this mask<br>
like I'm drowning and melting in a sweaty cask<br>
and it was the end for me and myself
