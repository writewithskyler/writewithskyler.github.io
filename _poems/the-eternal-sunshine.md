Flowery odor<br>
Urged to inhale<br>
Curvy figure<br>
Held you like an animal<br>
Glossy skin, transparent body<br>
You’re in me, wired and fired<br>
The world spun around me

Crawledd on the floor<br>
Slipped through the bedsheet<br>
Foggy, cold and cozy<br>
I closed my eyes, saw the light<br>
Why didn’t you tell me?<br>
All of these were for what’s in me<br>
Can’t you reverse time?<br>

The next day I woke up<br>
My body felt no soul<br>
Then I saw it in the mirror<br>
Devoured and thrashed<br>
“Ring… Ring…” The doorbell rang<br>
I opened the door<br>
There was my death, the eternal sunshine!
