Cold evening, a blizzard was striking<br>
A man with a gun shot an innocent<br>
Blissful moment, floating in ecstasy<br>
He was sleeping; I pushed him down,<br>
dragged him along the floor as he screamed

A snake in the backyard of a house, slithering through the grass<br>
A woman was watching it with malice in her eyes<br>
I was jealous that I had lost to her, so I commanded the snake,<br>
“Crawl into her house, crush her neck, swallow her whole.”<br>
I observed it as if I were looking at a piece of art in a gallery.

Those bluebloods with radiant skin<br>
Believed they would glow forever.<br>
But nothing lasts forever.<br>
I sowed a piece of me in them,<br>
Soothed them with wine, held them captive.

I sat in a tree in the suburbs<br>
Looking for another commotion.<br>
Everything was quiet and calm; I didn’t like it.<br>
A luster from the east bloomed like a firefly.<br>
I flew towards it like a rocket.

A snow-white heart, velvet hands,<br>
I couldn’t hold back from saying,<br>
“Come to me, and hold me tight.<br>
You can never escape from my string.<br>
And I, the devil, will be watching over you.”
