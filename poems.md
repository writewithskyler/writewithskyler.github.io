## The Phantom Sound – an Inner Voice That Chains You {#phantom-sound}

![A man in a dark hoodie stands with his back turned in a misty forest, facing a faint shadowy figure in the distance.](/assets/phantom-sound.jpg)

My head is stuffed with mud,<br>
My eyes are swollen and wrinkled,<br>
While I run in my mind nonstop.<br>
Somewhere in the middle, I heard a voice<br>
Say, “Hey! It’s me, the one you wanted to leave behind.”<br>
The phantom sound that imprisoned me long ago

“Between sleep and wakefulness,<br>
My mind wanders at night<br>
To look for a cage to keep me chained.<br>
I said, ‘Don’t.’ My mind said, ‘Won’t.’<br>
It is so sneaky that it tricked me,<br>
Played some evil games with me,<br>
And now I’m under control.”

With the strangeness in the air,<br>
The sadness in the rain,<br>
The happiness in the flowers,<br>
I slipped and ran away through the woods<br>
Faster than I could, as if a pack of wolves<br>
Were chasing me to devour my body.<br>
I gave my all to push a little more,<br>
I finally escaped, or so I thought.
