Your perception triggers your reaction, regardless of the circumstances. Perception-based captures the event, like a camera. No explanation, just what is seen/felt. Stream of consciousness captures the flow of thoughts. No order, just the impact flowing out. It provides raw material of mental flow that perception methods twist into sensory disruption.

## Perception-based

Perception-based methods are how reality is being perceived. The core techniques explore how perspective can alter reality. It makes the reader experience rather than explain, because the poem is rooted in reality, but through the poet’s perception, so the reader brings their experience to it. Perception-based also focuses on how readers interpret texts through their senses, emotions, and subjective viewpoint

One of the core techniques is Negative capability. It depicts a scenario as it was observed by the subject. There will be no truth other than the perspective of the subject. The technique ends unresolved because the poet shows probability and uncertainty.

## Stream of Consciousness

Stream of consciousness is a literary style used to capture the flow of a character’s thoughts. It has incomplete ideas, no traditional structure or filter. The impact of an event triggers the flow of consciousness. It is often fragmented, non-linear and disjointed, reflecting the spontaneous and chaotic nature of human thoughts. It is just the continuous rush of impressions, memories, worries, and a sudden change of awareness each second.

## Perception-based vs Stream of Consciousness

- In perception-based, the author uses techniques intentionally to shape the perspective and the poem’s direction and outcome. But in stream of consciousness, the speaker’s mind flows freely with associative leaps.
- Perception-based reshapes the reader’s mind and makes them perceive the reality like how the technique reshaped the reality. In contrast, readers recognize the true nature of the chaotic thoughts in the stream of consciousness.
- The reader feels alienation, ambiguity, and perceptual flips in perception-based. Whereas in stream of consciousness, the reader feels mental chaos and immersion in one mind.

## How They Work Together

The poet uses the raw material from stream of consciousness in perception to twist reality. It creates tension between reality and imagination that alters the reader’s thinking. The mix of fragmentation, chaos, ambiguity and internal and external dissociation can feel overwhelming and disorienting to the reader. The disorientation comes from experiencing both a twisted reality and a chaotic mind at the same time. Perception brings creativity and imagination, while stream of consciousness brings the human story—together they elevate the poem.

## Conclusion

Perception-based and stream of consciousness can be complex methods, but they talk about something very deep in human nature and human feelings. Exploring these methods can be very useful for people who are interested in poetry. However, regardless of the advanced technique, expressing our feelings and thoughts through poetry brings out the authenticity of an artist.
